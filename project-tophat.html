Overview & Ownership

End-to-end ownership of a flagship ₹20L composite fairing program

Executed at IC&SR, IIT Madras (sponsored by Indian Railways under the HyTech Center of Excellence for Hyperloop Technology) between September 2023 and April 2024 (building on initial baseline CFD benchmark validation from February to August 2023), I directed the end-to-end engineering and manufacturing of the ₹20 lakh Hyperloop Top Hat composite aerodynamic fairing.

The objective was to design, tool, and manufacture a lightweight, aerodynamically contoured 3.0 m × 1.1 m × 1.1 m composite shell capable of reducing aerodynamic drag by 35% (to Cd ≈ 0.25), withstanding operational handling loads, and integrating cleanly onto the levitation pod chassis within strict mass (60 kg) and budget limits.

The completed fairing was integrated at the 8.6 m test facility at NCCRD, IIT Madras, and showcased in operation to the Hon'ble Minister of Railways in March 2025.

Final assembled Top Hat composite fairing mounted on the Hyperloop pod at NCCRD, IIT Madras, ready for integration testing inside the 8.6 m vacuum test facility.
02

Engineering Narrative

Aerodynamic optimization via ANSYS Fluent CFD

I surfaced the 3.0 m × 1.1 m × 1.1 m aerodynamic body in SOLIDWORKS, balancing external pressure recovery, nose-to-tail fineness ratio, composite draft angles, and subsystem packaging clearances.

The external contour was refined across 20+ CFD iterations in ANSYS Fluent using the k-ω SST turbulence model, hexa-core meshes, and y+ calibrated boundary-layer prism inflation across 15+ operating conditions, reducing aerodynamic drag by 35% to Cd ≈ 0.25.

Prior to full-scale tooling release, I validated solver accuracy within 5% of published wind-tunnel drag data on a reference Tesla Model S benchmark and fabricated a 1:30 scale 3D-printed rapid prototype for physical geometry verification.

1:30 scale 3D-printed prototype used for physical verification of the aerodynamic profile prior to full-scale CNC pattern and mould commitment.
CNC-machined MDF master pattern fabricated for composite mould creation — the tooling foundation of the Top Hat fairing.

From CNC MDF master pattern to 60 kg production composite

I directed the complete CNC MDF master pattern and composite female mould fabrication workflow to ensure surface accuracy and repeatable part release.

I supervised the tailored glass-fiber/epoxy hand lay-up and vacuum bagging process using unidirectional (UD) and bidirectional (BD) fabric plies oriented at 0°/45°/90° to maximize panel buckling stiffness at minimal wall weight. The finished production shell achieved its target mass of 60 kg.

Quality assurance covered CNC pattern dimensional checks, resin cure monitoring, trim-line inspection, and automotive-grade surface finishing.

Multi-vendor execution & pod integration

I coordinated 5+ external suppliers spanning CNC pattern machining, composite mould tooling, glass-fiber lamination, automotive painting, logistics, and final pod integration at NCCRD, IIT Madras.

During mechanical integration, the fairing mounting brackets and structural hardpoints were aligned with the pod chassis frame, maintaining full clearance around the Linear Induction Motor (LIM), battery pack, and onboard telemetry harnesses.

Final fit-check and track readiness verification were completed in front of the 8.6 m vacuum test tube facility.

Deployment & Recognition

Hon'ble Minister of Railways showcase validates the engineering

Delivered under IC&SR, IIT Madras (sponsored by Indian Railways — HyTech Center of Excellence for Hyperloop Technology) between September 2023 and April 2024, the Top Hat became the program's first full-scale operational composite aerodynamic shell and was showcased during the official visit of the Hon'ble Minister of Railways in March 2025.

This milestone validated the complete engineering workflow — from ANSYS Fluent CFD optimization and CNC master patterning to tailored composite lay-up and pod integration.
