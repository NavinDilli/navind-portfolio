About

I am a Mechanical R&D Lead with a track record of taking ambiguous, high-stakes engineering challenges and delivering deployable, first-of-kind systems.

At Tutr Hyperloop, I progressed from CFD Engineer to Project Engineer to Lead — Digital Solution, building the organization's aerodynamic simulation capability from scratch, then leading two major hardware programs (₹20L Top Hat, ₹22L Vacuum Chamber), and now establishing its first Digital Engineering practice with real-time Digital Twins for predictive maintenance.

My work spans the full engineering lifecycle: aerodynamic optimization via CFD (ANSYS Fluent), composite manufacturing (carbon fiber & glass fiber lay-ups), structural FEA (ANSYS Mechanical), instrumentation and data acquisition (NI DAQ, strain gauges), and real-time simulation (MATLAB, Simulink, Raspberry Pi). I have coordinated 5+ vendors, managed ₹42L+ in program budgets, and presented results to the Hon'ble Indian Railway Minister.

I hold an M.E. in Aerospace Technology from Anna University — MIT Campus, where my research focused on transonic flow control, and a B.E. in Mechanical Engineering from Agni College of Technology.
