About

I am a Lead — Digital Solutions & Aerospace R&D Engineer with a track record of taking ambiguous, high-stakes engineering challenges and delivering deployable, first-of-kind hardware and virtual twin systems.

Across TuTr Hyperloop and IC&SR, IIT Madras (Indian Railways – HyTech Center of Excellence for Hyperloop Technology), I progressed from Graduate R&D Trainee (CFD) to Project Engineer — Aerospace R&D to Lead — Digital Solutions. After establishing baseline external aerodynamic CFD workflows from scratch, I spearheaded two flagship composite hardware programs at IIT Madras HyTech (the ₹20L Hyperloop Top Hat aerodynamic fairing and the ₹22L Carbon-Fiber Vacuum Pressure Chamber) before leading TuTr Hyperloop's Digital Engineering practice.

My work bridges physical hardware and real-time virtual simulation: real-time Structural & Battery Digital Twins (<100 ms latency with 120 Ω strain gauge Wheatstone bridges, NI USB-6003 DAQ, and Raspberry Pi 5 + INA226 over I²C), PyAnsys FEA batch automation for virtual load sensing, 1D Simscape electro-thermal plant modeling with closed-loop PID cooling control for Linear Induction Motors (LIM) and Metro HVAC systems, and full-scale composite manufacturing. I have coordinated 5+ vendors, managed ₹42L+ in R&D budgets, and showcased operational hardware to the Hon'ble Minister of Railways.

I hold an M.E. in Aerospace Technology from Anna University — MIT Campus, where my research focused on transonic shock-boundary-layer flow control, and a B.E. in Mechanical Engineering from Agni College of Technology.
